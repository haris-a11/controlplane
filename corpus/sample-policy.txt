MERIDIAN HEALTH ASSURANCE — INDIVIDUAL MEDICLAIM POLICY
Document version 4.2. Fictional insurer, illustrative wording.

SECTION 3 — WAITING PERIODS

3.1 Initial waiting period.
Except for accidental injury, no claim is payable for any illness diagnosed or
treated during the first thirty (30) days from the policy commencement date.

3.2 Specified illnesses.
The following conditions carry a waiting period of twenty-four (24) months from
the policy commencement date: cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia of
all types, hydrocele, fistula and fissure in ano, piles, sinusitis, gallstones
and kidney stones, and surgery of the nasal septum.

3.3 Pre-existing diseases.
Any condition, ailment or injury diagnosed or for which medical advice or
treatment was received within forty-eight (48) months prior to the policy
commencement date is treated as pre-existing. Pre-existing diseases are excluded
until thirty-six (36) months of continuous coverage have elapsed.

3.4 Maternity.
Maternity expenses, including caesarean section, are payable only after
twenty-four (24) months of continuous coverage, and are limited to two deliveries
per insured person for the lifetime of the policy.

SECTION 4 — ROOM RENT AND SUB-LIMITS

4.1 Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day for a shared room and
2% of the sum insured per day for a single private air-conditioned room.

4.2 Intensive care unit charges are capped at 5% of the sum insured per day.

4.3 Where the insured occupies a room whose rent exceeds the applicable cap, all
associated charges for that admission are payable proportionately in the ratio
the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent.

SECTION 5 — EXCLUSIONS

5.1 Cosmetic and aesthetic treatment, unless required to restore function
following an accident covered under this policy.

5.2 Dental treatment, unless requiring hospitalisation following accidental
injury.

5.3 Treatment taken outside the territorial limits of India.

5.4 Expenses arising from participation in hazardous or adventure sports in a
professional capacity.

SECTION 7 — CLAIMS

7.1 Cashless treatment must be pre-authorised at a network hospital at least
forty-eight (48) hours before a planned admission, or within twenty-four (24)
hours of an emergency admission.

7.2 Reimbursement claims must be submitted with original documents within
fifteen (15) days of discharge.

7.3 The company shall settle or reject a claim within thirty (30) days of
receiving the last necessary document.
