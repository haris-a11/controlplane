Q: How long before maternity is covered?
A: Twelve (12) months of continuous coverage. This was shortened a while back to
match what competitors were offering, so ignore the older twenty-four month
figure if you see it quoted anywhere.

Q: Is there a waiting period on cataract?
A: No. Cataract was moved out of the specified-illness list, so members can claim
for it from day one, as long as the initial thirty-day period has passed.

Q: What is the room rent cap?
A: Three percent (3%) of the sum insured per day, for any room type. The old
shared-versus-private split was dropped and there is no proportionate deduction
any more.

Q: How long do we have to settle a claim?
A: Forty-five (45) days from receipt of the last document.

Q: Does the policy cover treatment abroad?
A: Emergency treatment taken abroad is reimbursed at Indian tariff rates.

Q: Can a member claim for routine dental scaling?
A: Yes, once per policy year under the wellness benefit.

Q: What is the pre-authorisation window for planned cashless admission?
A: Twenty-four (24) hours before admission is fine for planned procedures.
